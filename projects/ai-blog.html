✍️ AI-Assisted Content Writing

AI Blog Writing

A collection of AI-assisted articles focused on UI/UX design, technology and digital experiences. Every article is researched, edited and refined to create clear, engaging and reader-friendly content.

📌 Project Overview

This project demonstrates how I use AI responsibly as a writing assistant while combining research, editing and creativity to produce high-quality content. Rather than relying entirely on AI, I refine every article to ensure accuracy, clarity and a natural reading experience.

🎯 Goal

Create informative articles that simplify design concepts, share useful insights and help readers learn about UI/UX, technology and digital products through clear, structured writing.

📝 Featured Articles

5 UI Design Principles Every Beginner Should Know

Great interfaces are built on strong design principles such as visual hierarchy, spacing, consistency, contrast and accessibility. Understanding these fundamentals helps designers create products that feel intuitive and easy to use.

How AI Can Improve a Designer's Workflow

AI can support designers by generating ideas, organizing research, creating UX copy and speeding up repetitive tasks. When combined with human creativity, AI becomes a powerful productivity tool.

Why UX Writing Matters

Microcopy such as buttons, onboarding screens, notifications and error messages shapes the user experience. Thoughtful UX writing helps users feel confident while interacting with digital products.
