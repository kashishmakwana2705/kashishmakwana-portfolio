Our Story

Made with matcha, mochi & a whole lot of heart

Cute started as a tiny weekend stall with one matcha machine and a big dream. Today we're a cozy corner café where regulars come for their morning latte and stay for the pastel pastries.

Every recipe is made in small batches, every cup poured with a little extra care — because a good café should feel like a hug.
